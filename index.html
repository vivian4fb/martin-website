The practice

The first doctorate in holography, and a lifetime of work since.

Richardson completed the first PhD in Holography at the Royal College of Art in 1988. His research examined optical reduction — enlarging and shrinking images of the human form through complex optical systems recorded on silver-halide materials.

As Head of the Modern Holography Group at De Montfort University, Leicester, he taught a generation of students who went on to pioneer the medium. In 2023 the Science Museum, London, acquired his entire body of work for its permanent collection.
